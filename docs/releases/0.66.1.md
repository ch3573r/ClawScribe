# ClawScribe 0.66.1 preview

This Windows x64 preview is available for manual installation and to users who enable **Include prereleases** in update settings. The stable channel continues to offer 0.65.1.

## Changes since 0.65.1

- Scroll back during live transcription to read earlier speech. Scroll to the bottom or select **Jump to live transcript** to follow new speech again. The Listening indicator has more space above the recording controls.
- Open a saved citation to see the passage and play from its timestamp. The passage stays in view when the player loads, and closing the source preview returns focus to the original control.
- Recover interrupted transcripts when recording storage is unavailable. Queue audio imports, retry incomplete folder saves, and schedule portable library backups.
- Keep previous notes visible during regeneration, retry a failed translation from the English draft, and review follow-up actions and export destinations before sending.

## Known limitations

The preceding candidate passed a short installed microphone and system-audio check and saved a second recording in the same process. In a two-hour recording with live assistance off, eight expected system-audio markers fell below the verification threshold, including two that could not be found in the saved audio. The cause remains open. The assistance-on comparison did not run, so long-meeting capture continuity and the effect of assistance on memory remain unverified.

Local Standard Meeting Notes can omit an agreed task or present an unsupported relationship as fact. Both a 4B and a 9B local model made these errors on the same fictional meeting. Check commitments, owners and deadlines against the transcript before using a draft or exporting actions.

Installed checks on the preceding candidate covered transcript reload, scroll-back, and timestamp playback at 100% Windows scaling. Physical Teams or Webex reconnect and sleep/wake, human listening, and installed testing of the final spacing adjustment are outstanding. This preview is for evaluation.
