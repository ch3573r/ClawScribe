# ClawScribe 0.62.0 preview

This preview fixes defects in starting, stopping and quitting a recording, and
adds font and text size settings.

## Starting a recording

- The meeting title and the microphone and system-audio devices chosen for a
  manually started recording did not reach the recorder. Every such recording
  used the default devices and a generic "Meeting" name. They are now passed on.
  The same defect affected the device choice when reconnecting a device.
- A recording could not start while a retranscription, an import, speaker
  detection or Test this PC was running. Starting a recording, by hand or
  through call detection, now stops that job and a notice names it. The
  meeting's previous transcript and speaker labels are kept; start the job again
  afterwards. A job that is already saving its result finishes first.
- Speaker detection cannot be interrupted in the middle of its analysis. On long
  audio the recording start can therefore still fail with a "still stopping"
  message after 30 seconds. Start the recording again.
- If audio capture fails while the speech model is still loading, the start is
  now cancelled with an error. Before, the app showed a running recording that
  took no audio.

## Stopping and quitting

- Stop in the sidebar works from every page. Before, it only opened Home unless
  Home was already open.
- After a stop, the app opens the new meeting only if Home is open. On any other
  page you stay where you are, and the notice offers **View meeting**. The
  automatic summary starts in both cases.
- When capture stops because of an error, a notice that stays on screen says so
  and the app saves what was captured. Before, nothing was shown.
- **Quit** in the tray menu during a recording now stops the recording and saves
  the meeting before the app closes. It waits up to two minutes and then closes
  anyway; the audio and transcript files written so far stay in the recording
  folder. Windows sign-out and shutdown are not covered.

## Home

- The moving bars shown during a recording were random and did not reflect the
  audio. They are replaced by a still recording mark. A real level display needs
  further work in the recorder.
- One elapsed-time clock is shown instead of two. It follows the recorder and no
  longer restarts at 0:00 when you return to Home.

## Notices

Notices follow the light or dark theme and appear at the bottom right. Before,
they were light on the dark theme and covered the question field in Meeting
memory. At the bottom right a notice can still cover the right end of a wide
input while it is shown.

## Transcription language

**Settings → Transcription** has a language setting. Before, it was reachable
only with Whisper, from a dialog on Home.

- Whisper: automatic detection, automatic detection with translation to
  English, or a fixed language.
- Nemotron: the system language or a fixed language.
- Parakeet and hosted transcription detect the language themselves, so no
  selector is shown. A language stored earlier is displayed with **Reset to
  automatic**, because it is still saved with new recordings and used to choose
  the speaker-matching model.

The setting is locked during a recording.

## Summaries in other languages

A summary in a language other than English is written in English first and then
translated. The translation replaced English working terms such as rollout,
deployment or review with native words. It is now instructed to keep such terms
and to write the way a native-speaking colleague would. This has not been
measured on real meetings. Summaries produced through Codex or Claude Code may
follow their own language handling and were not checked.

## Appearance

- Three more interface fonts: Inter, Geist and Source Serif 4.
- **Transcript and summary font** sets a separate font for long text.
- **Installed font** uses any font installed on this PC, by name. A note appears
  when Windows does not have a font of that name.
- **Text size** scales text and controls together: 90, 100, 110 or 125 percent.
  At 125 percent the window needs to be wider than the usual 1000 pixel minimum
  for every page to fit.

## Updating and testing

The Windows x64 installer and updater version is **0.62.0**. In **Settings →
General → Updates**, enable **Include prereleases**. Finish recording and
saving before installing. The stable channel continues to offer 0.5.58.

These changes were checked with automated tests and by rendering the changed
pages with real library data. None of them was exercised in an installed build
with a real recording. Real-device checks remain pending: a manual start with a
title and chosen devices, a start during a retranscription, Stop from another
page, Quit during a recording, a capture failure, notices in both themes, text
size, and the items listed in the [0.61.1 preview notes](0.61.1.md).
