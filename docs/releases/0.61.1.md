# ClawScribe 0.61.1 preview

## Recording start

With live transcription, pressing Record loaded the speech model first and
opened the microphone and system audio only afterwards. That took 2 to 5
seconds on a test PC, and whatever was said meanwhile was neither recorded nor
transcribed. Audio capture now starts while the model loads. Speech from those
first seconds is transcribed as soon as the model is ready, so the first lines
appear a little late instead of being lost. Audio-only recordings were not
affected.

If the model cannot be loaded after capture has begun, the start is cancelled
and the seconds captured so far are deleted.

## Quiet speech

Parakeet dropped speech from quiet audio. On a recording at about -37 dBFS the
same clear sentences came back complete, cut in half or empty, depending on
where a segment began. Quiet segments are now raised to a normal level before
they are transcribed, in live recording, retranscription and import. Audio at a
normal level is not changed.

With the Parakeet Ultra model this recovered every affected stretch in testing.
With Lightning it recovered most of the lost text, but very short segments can
still come back empty.

## Known issue

On one test recording at a normal level, the first two-second speech segment
came back empty in retranscription with the Lightning model. The cause is not
established and this release does not address it.

## Updating and testing

The Windows x64 installer and updater version is **0.61.1**. In **Settings →
General → Updates**, enable **Include prereleases**. Finish recording and
saving before installing. The stable channel continues to offer 0.5.58.

Both changes were checked against recordings on a development PC, not in an
installed build. Real-device checks remain pending: the first seconds of a live
recording, a quiet meeting, and the items listed in the
[0.61.0 preview notes](0.61.0.md).
