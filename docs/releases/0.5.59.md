# ClawScribe 0.5.59 preview

This Windows GPU preview improves speaker detection and relabeling, makes summary
citations point at the cited segment, and lists action items so they can be
exported. It builds on [0.5.58](0.5.58.md). Stable remains 0.5.58.

## Speaker detection

- The default voice model is now NeMo TitaNet small with a grouping threshold of
  1.15. The model is about 40 MB and downloads the next time you run Detect
  speakers.
- Measured on seven four-person meetings from the AMI corpus (about 3 hours, CPU):
  in Auto the previous default found 10 to 114 speakers and got 54% of speech time
  wrong on average. The new default finds 3 to 9 speakers with 23% wrong. With the
  speaker count given, the error went from 50% to 29%.
- About half of the remaining error is missed speech, mostly people talking over
  each other.
- Detect speakers is in the transcript menu for every meeting with a recording. It
  no longer needs the Beta source attribution setting, and speaker labels show
  whenever a meeting has them.
- Detection still runs only when you start it.

## Speaker relabeling

- A speaker chip on a transcript row opens "Who said this?": pick a speaker from
  the meeting, add a new one, rename that speaker everywhere or remove the label.
- A speaker name in the timeline opens a rename form. It says how many segments
  the change applies to, or that the name merges with an existing speaker.
- Typing an existing name in another case joins that speaker.
- Chips and timeline lanes use the same colour for a speaker. Unlabelled rows stay
  neutral.

## Summaries

- A citation resolves to a passage of at most 10 seconds. Before, a citation could
  open up to 45 seconds before the cited sentence.
- The standard meeting template lists action items as bullets with owner and due
  date. Planner and To Do export read this form; the earlier table could not be
  exported.
- Unanswered questions go to a new Open questions section and no longer disappear
  from the action items.
- A section with nothing to report reads "None noted." without a placeholder table.
- A template you saved yourself keeps its own sections. Reset it to get the new
  ones.

## Interface

- The playback strip and the active transcript row follow the accent colour.
- Chat and live assistance show a short model name; the full identifier is in a
  tooltip.
- Semantic search model details open on click.

## Screens to check

- Detect speakers on a meeting with three or more people and on a two-person call,
  in Auto and with the count given, in English and in German.
- Relabel one row, add a new speaker, rename a speaker everywhere, merge two
  speakers, and remove a label. Check keyboard use and both themes.
- A new summary with the standard template: citations, action items, open
  questions, and one export to Planner or To Do.
- Playback with each accent colour.

## Validation, limitations and updates

Nothing in this release has been run on an installed build. Speaker detection was
measured on one English corpus with four speakers per meeting; German and
two-person calls are not measured, and one of the seven meetings scored worse with
the new threshold than with a lower one. The measurement scores raw detection and
does not include the app's own retry when Auto returns many very short speakers.
The relabeling popovers were rendered in a browser against a mocked backend in the
dark theme; the light theme, more than six speakers and failed saves were not
checked by rendering. The citation change was tested in code, not against a saved
meeting.

Known limits: speaker colours follow order of first appearance and can shift after
a merge; two label changes made at almost the same moment are not queued; wide
summary tables from other templates can still be clipped while chat is docked.

Still open: all of the above on an installed build, plus the acceptance carried
over from [0.5.58](0.5.58.md), [0.5.57](0.5.57.md) and [0.5.56](0.5.56.md).

The installer, runtime and updater version is 0.5.59. Finish recording and saving
before installation. Tauri updater signatures are separate from Windows publisher
signing. The release profile is windows-gpu: Whisper Vulkan and ONNX/sherpa
DirectML; meeting embeddings use CPU.
