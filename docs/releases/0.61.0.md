# ClawScribe 0.61.0 preview

## Settings

The settings menu is reorganised. **Add-ons** is split into **Microsoft 365**,
**Calendars**, **Confluence** and **Assistant access**, each with its own page
and a status dot in the menu. The **Beta** page is gone: its four switches are
on **Transcription → Options**. Links that pointed at the old pages open the new
ones.

**Privacy** is new. It lists what stays on this PC and what leaves it, derived
from your current settings: recordings, transcription, meeting search,
summaries, live assistance, exports, calendars, assistant
access and the update check. If a setting cannot be read, the page says so
instead of reporting that data stays local.

**Recording** now holds call detection. A signal check shows the microphone
level while the page is open; nothing is recorded. System audio is not measured
there.

**Transcription** lists Parakeet models in a table with download size and
status. **Test this PC** measures the model in use, and the result is kept for
that model. Other models show "Not tested" until they have been in use and
tested.

**Summary** has no Save button. Switches and lists save when you change them,
and text fields save when you leave the field or press Enter. Choosing another
provider only shows its settings: the previous provider stays in use until you
choose **Use**. Live assistance moved here from Meeting memory.

**Shortcuts** apply as soon as you press the new keys. **Diagnostics** shows
health as a list with problems first, and reports the same call check that
starts recordings.

## Sidebar

The sidebar shows the next timed calendar event with **Use for next
recording**, and groups meetings into Today, This week and Earlier. The status
is a single line under the recording button.

## Fixes

Call detection recognises a Teams call whose controls are labelled **Leave**
and **Mute mic** or **Unmute mic**. In 0.5.60 such a call was reported as "No
active call detected".

Calendar files and subscriptions can be up to 16 MiB instead of 2 MiB. A
published Outlook calendar with full details exceeds 2 MiB within a year.
ClawScribe drops event descriptions, comments and attachments before a
calendar is stored or shown, including for calendars imported earlier.

## Updating and testing

The Windows x64 installer and updater version is **0.61.0**. Version numbers
continue from 0.61.0; there will be no further 0.5.x releases. In **Settings →
General → Updates**, enable **Include prereleases**. Finish recording and
saving before installing. The stable channel continues to offer 0.5.58.

Real-device checks remain pending for this build: updating an existing
installation, call detection during a real Teams call, subscribing to a real
calendar, the signal check with real audio devices, switching summary
providers, and every settings page in the installed app. Earlier pending items
are listed in the [0.5.60 preview notes](0.5.60.md).
